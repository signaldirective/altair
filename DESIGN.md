# ALTAIR — Design Notes

Named after **Altair IV** (*Forbidden Planet*, 1956): an eerie alien world of
colossal ancient megastructures, dark monoliths, orbital rings, and dusty
rose-lit skies — surveyed through a mid-century analog operator console.

## Rules

- **Rose is the light.** Pink is a beam or lens flare, never a flat neon wash.
- **Charcoal is the structure.** Keep large surfaces dark and monumental.
- **Warm metal balances cold steel.** Champagne beige and icy silver-blue
  define the expedition hardware and alien horizon.
- **Green is only a signal.** Retain it as a restrained phosphor indicator,
  not the dominant accent.

## Art

- Wallpaper direction: cinematic, textured 3D alien landscapes — monumental
  abstract structures, orbital rings, dusty warm light, pink beams, and
  atmospheric grain. `backgrounds/01.jpg` is the user-provided reference;
  `02.jpg`–`06.jpg` are AI-generated companions in the same grade.
- Unlock console: a mid-century operator terminal (beige machine housing,
  restrained CRT phosphor, analog instruments).
